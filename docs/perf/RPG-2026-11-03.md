# RPG click speed, 2026-11-03

**Machine:** AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D, CachyOS (Linux 7.3). **Build:** `--release`. **Harness:**
`cargo run --release -p feat-rpg --example clickbench`: a file SQLite database (WAL,
synchronous=NORMAL, as in production) with 30 players with history; 400 clicks per kind, each
one full transaction (load the player, run the rules, save, build the screen and its
Discord components). This is the bot's own work only; Discord's delivery and the reply's round
trip come on top and are timed live on the dashboard (RPG → Test).

| Click | p50 | p95 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Open the overview | 29 µs | 50 µs | 120 µs |
| Open the forge | 33 µs | 43 µs | 55 µs |
| Open the temple | 41 µs | 55 µs | 134 µs |
| Hunt turn | 38 µs | 67 µs | 139 µs |

Budget from the plan: 5 ms at p95. Every click answers with one `UpdateMessage` (v2 answered
with a deferred update and then an edit: two trips to Discord).

The banner is not drawn during a click: Discord fetches its signed address, the bot draws it
once (p50 53 ms, max 58 ms, 86 KB) and serves it from memory after that.
